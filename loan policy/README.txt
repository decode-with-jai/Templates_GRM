LOAN POLICY WEBSITE

1. Open index.html in any browser.
2. To change the redirect destination, search for:
   const REDIRECT_URL = 'https://example.com';
3. Replace https://example.com with your destination URL.
4. Upload index.html to your hosting public_html folder.
5. Replace the og-image.jpg URL in the meta tags with your own social sharing image.

Important:
Use a transparent destination and clearly disclose any affiliate or partner relationship.
