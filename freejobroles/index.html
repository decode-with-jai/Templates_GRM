FAQ

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about using FreeJobRoles — for both job seekers and hirers.

Still have questions?
How do I get started on FreeJobRoles?
Getting started is completely free. Simply click "Get Started", create your profile with your skills and experience, and start browsing thousands of opportunities immediately. No credit card required. You can be applying for jobs within minutes of signing up.
Is FreeJobRoles free for freelancers?
Yes, our Starter plan is completely free forever. You can browse 1,000+ jobs monthly and submit up to 3 applications per month at no cost. Upgrade to Professional ($29/month) for unlimited applications, AI matching, and priority visibility to employers.
How does the payment protection work?
We use an escrow-based system. When you start a project, the client funds the escrow. You complete the work, the client approves it, and the funds are released to you. If there's a dispute, our team reviews the work and mediates a fair resolution. You're always protected.
How are freelancers verified?
Every freelancer goes through a multi-step verification process: identity verification via government ID, skills assessment through standardized tests, portfolio review by our expert team, and reference checks where applicable. The blue verified badge means we've confirmed their credentials.
Can I post jobs for free as a company?
Companies can post up to 3 job listings per month on our free plan. For unlimited job posts, branded company pages, advanced applicant tracking, and dedicated support, our Enterprise plan starts at $99/seat/month. Contact our sales team for custom pricing for large organizations.
What makes FreeJobRoles different from Upwork or Fiverr?
Unlike other platforms, we focus on quality over quantity with our rigorous verification system. We offer both project-based freelancing and full-time/part-time employment in one place. Our AI matching means less time searching and more time working. Plus, our fees are significantly lower — freelancers keep up to 92% of their earnings.
