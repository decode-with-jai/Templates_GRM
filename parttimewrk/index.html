Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I get started on ParTimework?

Simply sign up with your email, create a profile, and you're ready to start. For freelancers, add your skills and portfolio. For employers, post a job and start receiving proposals from qualified candidates.

Is it really free to use ParTimework?

Yes! Our Basic plan is completely free forever. You can browse jobs, create a profile, and send proposals at no cost. Premium plans offer additional features like unlimited proposals and priority support.

How does payment work?

Employers fund a secure escrow account before work begins. Once work is complete and approved, payment is released to the freelancer. We use industry-standard security measures to protect both parties.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, PayPal, bank transfers, and cryptocurrency depending on your location. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Can I work on multiple projects at once?

Absolutely! Many freelancers manage multiple projects simultaneously. You can set your availability and workload preferences on your profile.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

We have a dispute resolution process to protect both freelancers and employers. If you're unsatisfied, you can request revisions or file a dispute for our support team to review.
